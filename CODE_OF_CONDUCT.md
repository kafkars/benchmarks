# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want technical disagreement to be direct, evidence-based, and respectful.
Participants are expected to make room for different experience levels,
criticize ideas rather than people, protect private information, and accept
clear maintainer decisions about project scope.

This project compares implementations, some of which people have spent years
building. Comparison is not a licence for contempt. Criticize a measurement, a
method, or a result; do not disparage the authors of the software being
measured.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project chat, events,
and private communication undertaken as a project representative.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `shawn@zsumz.com` with
`[kafka-benchmarks conduct]` in the subject. Do not force an affected person to
disclose details publicly. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a private warning, limit
participation, impose a temporary suspension, or permanently exclude a
participant. Decisions consider severity, pattern, impact, and the safety of the
community. Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation.
